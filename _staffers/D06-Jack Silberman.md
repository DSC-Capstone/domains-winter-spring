bio: |
  Dr. Jack Silberman is a Continuing Lecturer at the University of California San Diego (UC San Diego), with multidisciplinary academic appointments and teaching responsibilities spanning the Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering (MAE), the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE), and the Halıcıoğlu Data Science Institute (HDSI). He is also a faculty member of the Contextual Robotics Institute, where his teaching and research interests focus on Physical AI and autonomous systems.

  His teaching encompasses autonomous vehicles, robotics perception and navigation, artificial intelligence and machine learning, computer vision, and AI at the edge. He also leads multidisciplinary capstone projects that challenge students to design, develop, and improve race-grade autonomous vehicles, as well as intelligent systems for Quality of Life Technology (QoLT) applications.

  A passionate educator and mentor, Dr. Silberman mentors and coaches hundreds of students each year in advanced robotics and AI projects. Under his mentorship, the UC San Diego AI Racing Team achieved international recognition in the Indy Autonomous Challenge (IAC), demonstrating autonomous driving capabilities at speeds of up to 165 mph. He has also mentored student teams competing in the Autonomous Karting Series (AKS), RoboSub, and RobotX, as well as teams developing award-winning assistive technologies, including an affordable smart wheelchair and smart glasses for people with visual impairments. Across these initiatives, he emphasizes engineering excellence, interdisciplinary collaboration, and the practical application of artificial intelligence to real-world challenges.

  Beyond academia, Dr. Silberman brings more than 25 years of professional experience in automation, robotics, and advanced engineering. He holds a Ph.D. in Mobile Field Robotics from Carnegie Mellon University, a master's degree from the Pontifical Catholic University of Rio de Janeiro (PUC-Rio), and has conducted postgraduate robotics research in England. His industry experience spans educational robotics, NASA-sponsored mobile field robotics, and leadership roles in automation engineering for major companies in the semiconductor, biotechnology, medical device, and pharmaceutical industries.

description: |
  Technology with a Purpose: Intelligent Systems for Quality of Life (QoLT)
  What if the technology you develop could help someone become more independent, overcome a physical limitation, or improve their everyday life?

  Join a multidisciplinary capstone project focused on Quality of Life Technology (QoLT), where Artificial Intelligence, Robotics, and Human-Centered Engineering come together to address meaningful challenges in healthcare, accessibility, and assistive technology.

  Working with Dr. Jack Silberman and the UC San Diego Contextual Robotics Institute, you will explore and develop intelligent systems that empower people through innovative engineering solutions.

  What will you build and learn?

  Assistive Robotics: Design and develop intelligent mobility solutions, including affordable smart wheelchairs and robotic assistance systems.

  AI and Computer Vision: Build systems that recognize objects, understand environments, and provide context-aware assistance.

  Wearable Intelligence: Explore smart glasses and wearable technologies to assist people with visual impairments.

  Human-Machine Interaction: Develop intuitive interfaces that allow people to interact naturally with intelligent systems.

  Embedded and Edge AI: Deploy AI models on compact, low-power computing platforms for real-time applications.

  Prototyping and System Integration: Combine sensors, electronics, mechanical systems, software, and AI into functional prototypes.

  Why join?

  You will work on real-world problems where engineering innovation has a direct human impact. From assistive mobility to intelligent wearable devices, your ideas can become technologies that improve independence, accessibility, and quality of life.

  Build technology that matters. Engineer solutions that empower people!
