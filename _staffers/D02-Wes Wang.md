description: |
  Engineering estimates for government proposals depend on several things at once: reading the RFP, recalling similar past projects, checking current supply-chain conditions, positioning against competitors and meeting compliance requirements such as FAR/DFARS. Today this work is manual, slow and inconsistent from one estimator to the next. This project builds a multi-agent system in which specialized agents each handle one source: RFP requirement extraction, analogous projects and prior estimates, supply-chain pricing and lead times, competitive analysis, and regulatory compliance. An orchestrator then combines their outputs into a traceable estimate. Every cost element links back to its supporting evidence, so estimators can audit, adjust and defend the result.
